How to add to Gmail

  1. Click "Copy Moderate Signature" or "Copy Bold Signature" above.
  2. Open Gmail → click the gear ⚙ top-right → See all settings.
  3. Go to General tab → scroll to Signature section.
  4. Click Create new, give it a name, then paste (Cmd+V) into the editor box.
  5. Scroll down and hit Save Changes.
  6. Compose a new email — your signature appears automatically.

How to add to Hashnode blog posts

  1. Open your Hashnode post editor.
  2. At the bottom of the post, click the / command menu → choose HTML block.
  3. View this page's source (Cmd+U), copy everything between MODERATE SIGNATURE START and MODERATE SIGNATURE END.
  4. Paste the HTML into the Hashnode HTML block.
  5. Readers on modern browsers will see the full styled signature with clickable links.
